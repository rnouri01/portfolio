About

My work moves between disciplines: interface design, three-dimensional modeling, motion, and the question of how messages hold their meaning across languages. That range isn't incidental — it's method. Understanding how a product works across languages and cultures makes for clearer, more accessible, more considered design.

I laid the foundation for this with my bachelor’s degree in Computer Visualistics at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, specializing in design, video games, and intercultural communication. For the past two years, I have been working in the field of UX design and localization, including software products for the European and Asian markets, with a focus on multilingual interfaces.

I like working where design meets language: on interfaces built to be localized, on visual communication that carries across cultures, and on projects where precision matters as much as creativity.
